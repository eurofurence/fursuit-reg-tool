Where the colour comes from

  • The fursuits. Each species carries its own palette, so the collection is a wall of colour instead of grey pills. The chrome stays dark and quiet, like a Steam profile where the art is loud and the page is not.
  • Achievements are tiered. Teal for the early ones, blue next, gold for the long hauls and purple for team codes, so the row reads at a glance instead of being sixteen identical ticks. Leaderboard medals keep gold, silver and copper, but only on the medal itself, not as a row wash.
  • The showcase. Profiles lead with the owner's fursuit as a wide banner, the way Steam leads with artwork.
  • One interface colour. #005953 for actions, active nav and progress. Content colour never leaks into buttons, so nothing competes with the primary action.

Still worth deciding

  • Rarity is recomputed. UserCatch::getFursuitRarity() ranks by how often a suit was already caught, so popular reads as Legendary and day one is all Common. Here a species' share of the event population decides the tier, so a Fenfonex among three is Legendary from the first minute. Thresholds are 0.2 / 0.5 / 1.2 / 2.5 percent of the badges at the event. The colour then drives the tile ring, the row tint and the species heading.
  • Emoji. The live achievement icons are emoji in PHP (Furedex5.php and friends). These become tier colour plus a lucide glyph.
  • Naming. "furédex" kept or dropped.
