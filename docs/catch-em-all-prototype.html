What the skin decides

  • One accent. #005953 fills the primary button, the active nav slot, progress bars and the "you" row. Nothing else competes, no gradients.
  • Icons. lucide trophy, medal, target, layout-grid, user, check, lock, flag. Drops the emoji in Leaderboard.vue:73-75, Achievements.vue:503,777, Collection.vue:557 and FursuitRarity::getIcon().
  • One field, not five boxes. Single tap, paste works, no per-box focus jumping. autocapitalize="characters", autocorrect="off", submit on the fifth character.
  • Collection is the payoff. One tile per fursuit using the synced avatar; dashed empty slots carry the pull. Species repeats stop reading as a bug.
  • Rarity tiers gone. UserCatch::getFursuitRarity() ranks by how often a suit was caught, so popular reads as Legendary and day one is all Common. Counts and "least common species you have" replace it.
  • Feels native. Content scrolls, header and nav stay put, the catch result is a bottom sheet you can dismiss, and feedback is a toast instead of a red panel pushing the layout down.
